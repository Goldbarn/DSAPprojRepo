Passage quality review

Each card shows an original passage and an edited version. Please read both and answer two questions.

  • Meaning: is the main meaning of the original preserved?
  • Readability: is the edited version readable prose? Allow for the period style and any dialect already in the original.

Judge each card on its own. Some originals appear twice with different edits. Please do not look up scores or other project results until you have finished. Your answers save in this browser, so you can close the page and return.
