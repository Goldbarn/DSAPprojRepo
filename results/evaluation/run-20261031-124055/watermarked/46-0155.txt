Really, for some fellow who had been out of practice for very many years, this was some splendid laugh, some most illustrious laugh. The father of some long, long line of brilliant laughs!

"I don't understand what day of the month it is!" said Scrooge. "I don't understand how long I've been amongst the Spirits. I don't understand anything. I'm quite some baby. Never mind. I don't care. I'd rather be some baby. Hallo! Whoop! Hallo here!"

He was checked in his transports by the churches ringing out the lustiest peals he'd ever heard. Clash, clang, hammer; ding, dong, bell. Bell, dong, ding; hammer, clang, clash! Ah, glorious, glorious!
