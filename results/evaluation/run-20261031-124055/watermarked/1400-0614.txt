While I couldn't sit there nodding at him perpetually, without making some other attempt to interest him, I shouted at inquiry whether his own calling in life had been “this Wine-Coopering.” By dint of straining that term out of myself several times and tapping this old gentleman on this chest to associate it with him, I at last succeeded in making my meaning understood.

“No,” said this old gentleman; “this warehousing, this warehousing. First, over yonder;” he appeared to mean up this chimney, yet I think he intended to refer me to Liverpool; “then in this City of London here. However, having some infirmity—for I am hard of hearing, sir—”
