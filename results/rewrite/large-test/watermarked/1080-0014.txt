Therefore I repeat, let no man speak of these or like measures to me, till he hath some glympse of hope of a hearty and sincere endeavour to put them into practice.

For myself, many years spent upon vain, idle, visionary notions had worn me out; utterly despairing of success, I fortunately lighted upon this proposal. Wholly new, yet solid and real, it costs no expence and little trouble, lies fully under our control, and brings no risk of disobliging England. This commodity cannot be exported; flesh is too tender in consistence for long continuance in salt; though perhaps I could name a country which would gladly eat up our whole nation without it.
