“Well, dearies, how have you got on today? There was so much to do, getting the boxes ready to go tomorrow, that I didn’t come home to dinner. Has anyone called, Beth? How is your cold, Meg? Jo, you look tired to death. Come and kiss me, baby.”

Whilst asking these maternal questions Mrs. March slipped off her wet clothes, put on her warm slippers, then settled into the easy armchair, drawing Amy upon her lap, preparing to enjoy the happiest hour of her busy day. The girls flew about, each trying in her own way to make things comfortable. Meg arranged tea upon the table; Jo brought wood and set chairs, dropping, upsetting, and clattering everything she touched. Beth kept trotting between parlor and kitchen, quiet and busy, whilst Amy sat with folded hands, giving everyone directions.
