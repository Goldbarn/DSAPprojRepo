“I might have found consolation in imagining that the little people had sheltered my mechanism for me, were I less assured how little their bodies or intellects could accomplish. What dismayed me was this unsuspected power, intervening to make my invention vanish. Yet I felt assured of one thing: unless another age had produced its exact duplicate, my machine could never have travelled in time. Its levers were attached in a fashion I will show you later; removing them prevented tampering of that kind. Its movement and concealment were confined to space. But where, then, could it be?
