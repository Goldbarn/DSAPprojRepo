Assuming the blubber to be the skin of the whale; consider, then, how this skin, in a very large Sperm Whale, yields one hundred barrels of oil; consider, too, that this expressed oil accounts for only three fourths of its whole substance in quantity, or rather weight: thus you may conceive how enormous that living creature must be, when a mere portion of its mere integument yields such a lake of liquid as that. At ten barrels to the ton, you have ten tons net weight, yet this accounts for only three fourths of the substance of a whale's skin.
