Through the streets the Guardian of the Gates guided them to a big building exactly in the City's middle: the Palace of Oz, the Great Wizard. Before its door stood a soldier in green uniform, his beard long and green.

“Here are strangers,” said the Guardian of the Gates to him, “and they demand to see the Great Oz.”

“Step inside,” answered the soldier, “and I will carry your message to him.”
