Anne burst into tears, rushed to the hall door, and slammed it till the tins outside on the porch wall rattled in sympathy. Through the hall she fled, upstairs like a whirlwind. From above came a subdued slam: the east gable's door had closed with equal vehemence.

“Well, I don't envy you your job bringing _that_ up, Marilla,” said Mrs. Rachel with unspeakable solemnity.

Marilla opened her lips to offer she knew not what apology or deprecation. Her actual words surprised her then and ever afterwards.

“You shouldn't have twitted her about her looks, Rachel.”
