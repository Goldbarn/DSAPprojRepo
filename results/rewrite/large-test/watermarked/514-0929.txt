“Yes, if she saw you now. She hates lazy people. Why don't you do something splendid, and make her love you?”

“I did my best, but it was no use.”

“Graduating well, you mean? You ought to have done that much for your grandfather's sake. After spending so much time and money, failing would have been shameful, since everyone knew you could succeed.”

“I did fail, say what you will, for Jo wouldn't love me,” began Laurie, resting his head despondently on his hand.

“No, you didn't; someday you'll admit that. It did you good, proving you could accomplish something by trying. Set about some fresh task, and soon you'd be your hearty, happy self again, forgetting your trouble.”
