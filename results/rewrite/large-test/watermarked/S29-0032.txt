Yet grant that we were all of English descent; what does that amount to? Nothing. Now that Britain is our declared enemy, every other name and title vanishes: truly farcical, then, to call reconciliation our duty. William the Conqueror, first king of England's present line, was a Frenchman; half England's Peers descend from that same country. By this same reasoning, therefore, France ought to govern England.

Much hath been said of Britain joining her strength with that of the colonies, that together they might bid defiance to the world. This is mere presumption; war's fate is uncertain, nor have these expressions any meaning; this continent would never allow its population to be drained away in aid of British arms, whether in Asia, Africa, or Europe.
