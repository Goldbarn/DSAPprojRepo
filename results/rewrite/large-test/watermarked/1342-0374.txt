“If one could but go to Brighton!” observed Mrs. Bennet.

“Oh yes! if one could but go to Brighton! But papa is so disagreeable.”

“A little sea-bathing would set me up for ever.”

“And my aunt Philips is sure it would do _me_ a great deal of good,” added Kitty.

Lamentations like these continually resounded through Longbourn House. Elizabeth tried to find amusement in them, yet shame destroyed every sense of pleasure. She felt afresh how just Mr. Darcy's objections were; never before had she felt so ready to forgive his interference with his friend's plans.
