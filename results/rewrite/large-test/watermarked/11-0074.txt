Then both, bowing low, entangled their curls together.

Alice laughed so much that she ran back into the wood, afraid they would hear her. Next time she peeped out, the Fish-Footman had gone; his companion sat near the door upon the ground, staring stupidly skywards.

Alice approached the door timidly and knocked.

“You gain nothing by knocking,” said the Footman. “I have two reasons. First, because you and I are on the same side of the door; secondly, because with such a noise inside, nobody could possibly hear you.” Indeed, extraordinary noise _was_ going on within: continual howling and sneezing, with a great crash every now and then, as if a dish or kettle had broken into fragments.
