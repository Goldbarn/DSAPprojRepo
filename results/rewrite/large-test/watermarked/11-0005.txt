Down, down, down. Would the fall _never_ come to an end? “I wonder how many miles I've fallen by this time?” she said aloud. “I must be getting somewhere near the centre of the earth. Let me see: that would be four thousand miles down, I think” (you see, Alice knew several such things from her schoolroom lessons; though she could hardly show off her knowledge without anyone listening, saying it over gave her good practice) “yes, that's about the right distance, but then I wonder what Latitude or Longitude I've got to?” (Alice knew nothing about Latitude, or Longitude either; she merely thought them nice grand words to say.)
