“Attentively the jeweller examined the inn within, observing the apparent poverty of those about to sell him a diamond that seemed to have come from a prince’s casket.

“‘Relate your story, madame,’ said he. Doubtless he wished to profit by her husband’s absence, so that she could tell her story without his influence, and he could see whether their two recitals agreed.

“‘Oh,’ returned she, ‘it was a gift of heaven. In 1814 or 1815 my husband was a great friend of a sailor named Edmond Dantès. Though Caderousse had forgotten this poor fellow, the sailor had never forgotten him; at his death he left him this diamond.’
