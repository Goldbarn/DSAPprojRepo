Then, ready for their journey, they set off for the Emerald City. The Winkies gave them three cheers and many good wishes to carry with them.

You will remember that no road, not even a pathway, ran between the Wicked Witch’s castle and the Emerald City. When the four travelers went in search of the Witch, she saw them coming and sent the Winged Monkeys to bring them to her. Finding their way back through the big fields of buttercups and yellow daisies proved much harder than being carried. Of course they knew they must go straight east, toward the rising sun, so they set off in the right direction. But at noon, with the sun overhead, they could not tell east from west. That was how they became lost in the great fields. They kept walking, however. At night the moon came out and shone brightly. Among the sweet smelling yellow flowers they lay down and slept soundly until morning, all except the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman.
