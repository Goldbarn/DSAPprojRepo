6 Mr. Horner and Sir David Brewster describe (Philosophical Transactions, 1836, p. 65) a singular "artificial substance resembling shell." Fine, transparent, highly polished, brown-coloured laminae of peculiar optical properties collect inside a vessel where cloth, prepared first with glue, then lime, revolves rapidly in water. Compared with Ascension's natural incrustation, this substance is much softer, more transparent, richer in animal matter; yet here again we see how strongly carbonate of lime and animal matter tend to form a solid substance allied to shell.
