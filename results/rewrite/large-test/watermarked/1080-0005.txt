I therefore humbly offer for publick consideration, that twenty thousand of our computed hundred and twenty thousand children may be kept for breed, whereof one fourth only to be males. This exceeds our allowance to sheep, black cattle, or swine; my reason being, that these children seldom spring from marriage, which our savages little regard; thus one male may serve four females. The remaining hundred thousand, at a year old, may be sold throughout the kingdom to persons of quality and fortune; their mothers should always be advised to let them suck plentifully during the last month, to render them plump and fat for a good table. For an entertainment of friends, a child makes two dishes; for a family dining alone, its fore or hind quarter makes a reasonable dish, which, seasoned with a little pepper or salt, will be very good boiled on the fourth day, especially in winter.
