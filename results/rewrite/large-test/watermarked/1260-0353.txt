“Tall, a fine bust, shoulders sloping; her neck long and graceful: her olive complexion dark and clear; noble features; eyes rather like Mr. Rochester's, large, black, brilliant as her jewels. Then she had a fine head of hair, raven-black, arranged so becomingly: thick plaits crowning her head behind; in front, the longest, the glossiest curls I ever saw. She was all in pure white; over her shoulder, across her breast, lay an amber-coloured scarf, tied at her side, its long fringed ends descending below her knee. Her hair held an amber-coloured flower, too, contrasting well with her jetty curls.”
