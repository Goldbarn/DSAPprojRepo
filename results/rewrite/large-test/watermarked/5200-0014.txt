“What now, then?” Gregor asked himself, looking around in the darkness. Soon he discovered he could move no longer. This hardly surprised him; moving about on those spindly little legs until now seemed rather unnatural. He also felt fairly comfortable. His whole body ached, true, yet its pain seemed gradually to fade and would eventually disappear altogether. He could scarcely feel the decayed apple in his back, or its inflamed surroundings, entirely covered in white dust. He thought back to his family with emotion and love. If possible, his feeling that he must go away was stronger even than his sister's. Empty, peaceful rumination held him until he heard the clock tower strike three in the morning. He watched light slowly begin spreading outside his window too. Then, without his willing it, his head sank completely down; his final breath issued weakly through his nostrils.
