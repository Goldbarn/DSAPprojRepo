“Oh,” replied George, “don't you have no fear; WE'RE all square, we are. First, you've made a hash of this cruise--you'll be bold to deny that. Second, you let our enemy out o' this here trap for nothing. Why did they want out? I dunno, but it's pretty plain they wanted it. Third, you wouldn't let us attack them upon the march. Oh, we see through you, John Silver; you want to play booty, that's what's wrong with you. And then, fourth, there's this here boy.”

“Enough, too,” retorted George. “We'll all swing and sun-dry for your blundering.”
