“I don’t believe it,” said Josie flatly. “I don’t believe anybody could walk a ridgepole. _You_ couldn’t, anyhow.”

“Then I dare you to do it,” said Josie defiantly. “I dare you to climb up there and walk the ridgepole of Mr. Barry’s kitchen roof.”

Anne paled, but clearly only one course remained. She walked toward the house; a ladder leaned against its kitchen roof. “Oh!” said all the fifth-class girls, partly excited, partly dismayed.

“Don’t you do it, Anne,” entreated Diana. “You’ll fall off and be killed. Never mind Josie Pye. It isn’t fair to dare anybody to do anything so dangerous.”
