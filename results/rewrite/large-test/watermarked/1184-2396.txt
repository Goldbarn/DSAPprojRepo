“Truly, my daughter,” he replied with a smile, “you are everything you boast of being, save one thing; I shall tell you that without haste, preferring to let you guess.”

Eugénie regarded Danglars in great surprise: could he dispute a single flower from her crown of pride, with which she had decked herself so superbly?

“My daughter,” continued the banker, “you have perfectly explained what sentiments influence a girl like you, determined never to marry; now I must explain what motives influence a father like me, determined that his daughter shall marry.”
