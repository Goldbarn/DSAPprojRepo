“‘If she had offered to come aboard I really think I would have tried to shoot her,’ said the man of patches, nervously. ‘Every day for a fortnight I have risked my life keeping her out of the house. One day she came in. She kicked up a row about those miserable rags I had found in the storeroom for mending my clothes. I wasn’t decent. At least that must have been it, for she talked like a fury to Kurtz for an hour, pointing at me now and then. I don’t understand this tribe’s dialect. Luckily for me, I fancy Kurtz felt too ill that day to care, or there would have been mischief. I don’t understand.... No, it’s too much for me. Ah, well, it’s all over now.’
