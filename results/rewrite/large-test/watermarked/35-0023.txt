“You have told Blank, Dash, and Chose about the machine?” he said to me, leaning back in his easy-chair as he named his three new guests.

“But the thing’s a mere paradox,” said the Editor.

“I can’t argue tonight. I don’t mind telling you this tale, but I can’t argue. I will,” he went on, “tell you what I have lived through, if you like, but you must refrain from interruptions. I want to tell it. Badly. Most of it will sound like lies. So be it! It’s true, every word of it, all the same. At four o’clock I was in my laboratory. Since then … I’ve lived eight days … days such as no human being ever lived before! I’m nearly worn out, but I shan’t sleep till I’ve told you everything. Then I shall go to bed. But no interruptions! Is it agreed?”
