I planned my walk to reach the gate at my old time. After ringing the bell with an unsteady hand, I stood facing away from the gate, trying to recover my breath and quiet my heart's beating. I heard the side-door open, then steps approaching across the courtyard; yet I affected not to hear, even as the gate moved on its rusty hinges.

At last a touch on my shoulder made me start and turn. I started much more naturally then, finding before me a man dressed in sober grey. The last man I should have expected to see serving as porter at Miss Havisham's door.
