“Why did he wish it? What use could you have for Hindostanee?”

“He intended that I should go with him to India.”

“Ah! now I come to the root of the matter. He wanted you to marry him?”

“That is a fiction, an impudent invention to vex me.”

“I beg your pardon, it is literally true: he asked me repeatedly, urging his point with as much stiffness as ever you could show.”

“Miss Eyre, I repeat it, you can leave me. Must I say the same thing again and again? Why remain pertinaciously perched on my knee after I have given you notice to quit?”
