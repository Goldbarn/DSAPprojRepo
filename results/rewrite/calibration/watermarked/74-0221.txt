Not a sound. You could have heard a pin drop. That silence went on; face after face, the teacher searched for signs of guilt.

Another denial. Tom's uneasiness became more intense through this slow torture. The teacher looked over his ranks of boys—considered a while, then faced his class of girls:

Another negative. Next came Becky Thatcher. Tom was shaking from head to foot with excitement, seeing no hope at all.

“Rebecca Thatcher” [Tom glanced at her face—white with fear]—“did you tear—no, look me right in my face” [her hands rose in appeal]—“did you tear this book?”
