“Any other boy!” Tom thought, grating his teeth. “Any boy in this whole town but that Saint Louis smarty that thinks he dresses so fine and is aristocracy! Oh, all right, I licked you on that first day you ever saw this town, mister, and I'll lick you again! You just wait till I catch you out! I'll just take and—”

Then he went through his motions of thrashing some imaginary boy—pummelling the air, kicking, gouging. “Oh, you do, do you? You holler 'nough, do you? Now, then, let that learn you!” So his imaginary flogging came to an end, to his satisfaction.
