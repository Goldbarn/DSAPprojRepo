“Then what did you do?” asked Holmes.

“I went home to Saxe-Coburg Square, where I sought the advice of my assistant. He could offer me no help at all. He could tell me just that, were I patient, I would hear through the post. That was not quite good enough for me, Mr. Holmes. I did not want to lose such a place without some struggle, and since I had heard that you were kind enough to give advice to poor people who were in need of it, I came right over to you.”

“You did very wisely,” said Holmes. “Your case is a very remarkable one, and I would be happy to look into this matter. From what you have told me I think that graver issues might hang from this than at first sight appear.”
