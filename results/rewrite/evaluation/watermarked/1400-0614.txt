Since I could not sit nodding at him forever without some further effort at interesting him, I shouted out to inquire whether his calling had been “the Wine-Coopering.” After several times straining that term out of myself, each time tapping that old gentleman on his chest to fix that upon him, I made myself understood at last.

“No,” said that old gentleman; “the warehousing, the warehousing. First, over yonder;” he appeared to mean up the chimney, though I think he intended Liverpool; “then in this City of London. However, having some infirmity—for I am hard of hearing, sir—”
