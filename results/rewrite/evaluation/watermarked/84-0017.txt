Such words, you may imagine, strongly excited my curiosity; yet that paroxysm of grief overcame this stranger's weakened powers, and many hours of repose, of tranquil conversation, were necessary to restore his composure.

After he had conquered his violent feelings, he appeared to despise himself as some slave of passion; quelling that dark tyranny of despair, he led me again to speak of myself. He asked about my early years. That tale was soon told, yet it awakened various trains of reflection. I spoke of my desire of finding a friend, of my thirst for some more intimate sympathy with a fellow mind than had ever fallen to me, then expressed my conviction that no man could boast of much happiness without this blessing.
