“Thank God,” said Mr. Lorry, clasping his hands, “that no one near and dear to me is in this dreadful town to-night. May He have mercy on all persons in danger!”

Soon after that, the bell at the great gate sounded. “They have come back!” he felt, listening where he sat. Yet no irruption came into that courtyard, though he had expected one; he could hear that gate clash again, then silence.

His nervousness and dread made him feel some vague uneasiness about the Bank, that great change would naturally awaken, given such feelings. That house was guarded, and he came to his feet to join those persons watching over it, when his door flew open; two figures ran in. At that sight he fell back, amazed.
