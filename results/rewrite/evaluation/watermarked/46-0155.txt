Really, for one that had been out of practice so many years, that was quite a splendid laugh, an illustrious laugh! Father of a great line of brilliant laughs!

"I have no idea what day of the month it is!" said Scrooge. "I have no idea how long I've been among the Spirits. I have no idea of anything. I'm quite a baby. Never mind. I don't care. I'd rather be a baby. Hallo! Whoop! Hallo here!"

His transports were checked by every church ringing out its lustiest peals he had ever heard. Clash, clang, hammer; ding, dong, bell. Bell, dong, ding; hammer, clang, clash! Oh, glorious, glorious!
