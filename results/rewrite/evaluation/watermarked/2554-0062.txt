“You could settle this yourself, Lizaveta Ivanovna,” that huckster was saying aloud. “Come over about seven to-morrow. They too would be here.”

“To-morrow?” said Lizaveta, slowly, thoughtfully, like one unable to decide.

“Upon my word, you are in such fear of Alyona Ivanovna,” gabbled the huckster's wife, a lively little body. “I look at you, you are like some new babe. She is not even your own sister--just a step-sister, yet what a hand she has over you!”

“Yet this time don't tell Alyona Ivanovna,” her husband said, interrupting; “that's my advice, just come over without asking. That would be worth your while. After that your sister herself might have some notion.”
