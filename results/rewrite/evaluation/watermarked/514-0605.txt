That was a pictorial sheet. Jo examined its nearest work of art, idly wondering what fortuitous concatenation of circumstances needed this melodramatic illustration of some Indian in his war costume, tumbling over a precipice, his throat held by a wolf, while two infuriated young gentlemen, of tiny feet and big eyes, were stabbing each other close at hand, as some disheveled female fled behind them, her mouth open. Pausing to turn over his page, that lad saw her looking; with boyish good nature he offered her half of his paper, saying bluntly, “want to read it? That's a first-rate story.”
