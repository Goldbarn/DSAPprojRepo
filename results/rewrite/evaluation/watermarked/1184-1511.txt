“Did I not tell you just now that I was rich, Maximilian—too rich? I possess nearly 50,000 livres through my mother; my grandfather and grandmother, the Marquis and Marquise de Saint-Méran, will leave me as much, while M. Noirtier evidently intends to make me his heir. My brother Edward, who inherits nothing from his mother, would therefore be poor beside me. Now, had I taken the veil, all this fortune would have descended to my father, then in reversion to his son.”

“Ah, how strange that such a young, beautiful girl should be so avaricious.”
