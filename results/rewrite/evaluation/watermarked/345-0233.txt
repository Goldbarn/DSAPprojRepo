“To-morrow I want you to bring me, before night, some post-mortem knives.”

“Yes and no. I want to operate, though not as you think. Let me tell you now, yet tell this to none other. I want to cut off her head, then take out her heart. Ah! you a surgeon, yet so shocked! You, whom I have seen without tremble of hand or heart, do operations of life and death that make others shudder. Oh, but I must not forget, my dear friend John, that you loved her; nor have I forgotten, for it is I that shall operate, while you must only help. I would like to do this to-night, but for Arthur I must not; he will be free after his father's funeral to-morrow, when he will want to see her--to see _it_. Then, once she is coffined ready for the next day, you and I shall come while all sleep. We shall unscrew her coffin-lid, do our operation, then replace all, so that none know, save we alone.”
