“Caroline decidedly says that none of their party would come back into Hertfordshire this winter. I will read this to you.

“'After my brother left us yesterday, he imagined that his business at London might be concluded in three or four days; yet since we are sure that could not be, and at this same time convinced that once Charles gets to town he would be in no hurry to quit it, we have determined upon following him there, that he may not be obliged to spend his vacant hours at some comfortless hotel. Many of my friends are there for this winter: how much I would like to hear that you, my dearest friend, had any thought of joining that crowd, though of that I despair. I sincerely hope your Christmas in Hertfordshire may abound in all those gaieties that season generally brings, and that your beaux would be so many as to make you never feel that loss of three friends of whom we shall deprive you.'
