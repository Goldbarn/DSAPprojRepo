Thinking straight is now becoming a great effort for me. Just this nervous weakness, I suppose.

Then dear John held me in his arms, just carried me upstairs, set me on our bed, and sat reading to me till my head felt tired.

He said I was his darling, his comfort, all he had, and that I must look after myself for his sake, and keep well.

He says none but myself could help me out of this, that I must use my will, my self-control, and never let silly fancies run off with me.
