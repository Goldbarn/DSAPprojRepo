"Long Past?" said Scrooge: noting its dwarfish stature.

Scrooge could not, it may be, have told any person why, had he been asked; yet he felt some special desire to see that Spirit with his cap on, and begged him be covered.

"What!" exclaimed the Ghost, "would you so soon put out, with worldly hands, this light I give? Does it not suffice that you were one of those whose passions made this cap, that force me through whole trains of years to wear it low upon my brow!"

Scrooge, very reverently, disclaimed all thought of offence, as well as any knowledge of having ever "bonneted" that Spirit at any time of his life. He then made bold to inquire what had brought him there.
