Thirdly, Whereas each of a hundred thousand children, of two years old and upwards, could not be maintained at less than ten shillings per annum, this nation's stock would thereby rise fifty thousand pounds per annum, over and above that profit of some new dish, upon the tables of all gentlemen of fortune in this kingdom that have any refinement of taste. That money would also stay among our selves, these goods being entirely of our own growth and manufacture.

Fourthly, Those constant breeders, over that gain of eight shillings sterling per annum through the sale of their children, would be rid of that charge of maintaining them after their first year.
