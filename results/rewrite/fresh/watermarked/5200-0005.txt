This was how Gregor received his food each day now, first in the morning while his parents and their maid were asleep, then again after everyone had eaten at midday, as his parents would then sleep for some while too, and Gregor's sister would send their maid away on some errand. Gregor's father and mother surely did not want him to starve either, though it might have been more than they could stand to have any further experience of his feeding than being told about it, and his sister may have wanted to spare them what distress she could, as they were suffering enough.
