I stayed ten weeks at Maldonado, during which time I made a nearly perfect collection of its animals, birds, and reptiles. Before making observations about them, I would tell of some little excursion I made over to the river Polanco, about seventy miles to the north. As proof of how cheap everything is in this country, I may mention that I paid only two dollars per day, or eight shillings, for two men, together with some dozen riding-horses. My companions were armed with pistols and sabres; a precaution I felt rather unnecessary, but the first news we heard was that, the day before, some traveller from Monte Video had been found dead on the road, his throat cut. This happened close to a cross, the record of a former murder.
