“You know me, my dear friend? Think again. This is not your proper occupation. Think, dear friend!”

He would say nothing further. He would raise his eyes, briefly, on being requested; yet no persuasion could get one word out of him. He worked, and worked, and worked, in silence, while words fell upon him as they would have fallen upon some echoless wall, or upon the air. All that Mr. Lorry could find of hope was that he sometimes furtively looked up without being asked. That look held some faint expression of curiosity or perplexity--as though he tried to reconcile some doubts in his head.
