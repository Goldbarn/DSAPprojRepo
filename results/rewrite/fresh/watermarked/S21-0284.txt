Can you divine what my first thought was? Put into words, it was this: 'It seems, then, you escaped me once: there will be a different result this time!'

Evidently this creature was suffering unimaginable terrors. Think what it must have been to come awake in the midst of that voiceless hush, and look out over that grim congregation of the dead! What gratitude shone in his skinny white face at sight of a living form before him! How much more fervent this mute gratitude became when his eyes fell upon those life-giving cordials in my hands!
