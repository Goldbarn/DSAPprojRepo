“Mother, show Rodya that letter of Pyotr Petrovitch's,” said Dounia.

Pulcheria Alexandrovna, her hands trembling, gave him that letter. He received it with great interest, yet before opening it he turned some look of wonder upon Dounia.

“This is strange,” he said, slowly, like one with a new idea. “What am I making such a fuss for? What is all this about? Marry whom you like!”

He said this as if to himself, though aloud, then for some time looked at his sister, as though puzzled. He opened that letter at last, that same look of strange wonder on his face. Then, slowly, attentively, he began reading, going through it twice. Pulcheria Alexandrovna showed marked anxiety, while each of them expected something particular.
