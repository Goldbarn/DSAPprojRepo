“Oh, make haste, then, tell me; that has not happened for some time.”

“Well, he told me that Benedetto, that is considered a serpent of subtlety and a giant of cunning, is really just some very commonplace, silly rascal, altogether unworthy of those experiments that would be made on his phrenological organs after his death.”

“Bah,” said Beauchamp, “he played the prince very well.”

“Yes, in your eyes, Beauchamp, since you detest those unhappy princes, ever delighted at finding fault with them; though not in mine, for I can discover a gentleman through instinct, then scent out an aristocratic family like a very bloodhound of heraldry.”
