“I dare say she'd put them back if you asked her,” said someone.

“How could I, after that fuss?” began May, then stopped, for Amy's voice came over the hall, saying pleasantly...

“You may have them without asking, if you want them, and welcome. I was just thinking I'd offer them back, since they belong on your table rather than mine. Here they are, please have them, and forgive me if I was hasty in carrying them off that night.”

While she spoke, Amy gave back her contribution, with a nod and a smile, then hurried off again, feeling that a friendly thing was easier done than to stay and be thanked.
