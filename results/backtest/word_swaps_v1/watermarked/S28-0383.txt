To this second that is, to this pretended establishment of this common and state law by this Constitution, I answer, that they're expressly made subject “to such alterations and provisions as this legislature shall from time to time make about this same.” They're therefore at any second liable to repeal by this ordinary legislative power, and of course have no constitutional sanction. This just employ of this declaration was to recognize this ancient law and to remove doubts that might have been occasioned by this Revolution. This consequently can be considered as no part of some declaration of rights, that underneath our constitutions must be intended as limitations of this power of this government itself.
