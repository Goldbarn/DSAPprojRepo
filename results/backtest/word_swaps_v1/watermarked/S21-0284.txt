Can you divine what my first believed was? Put into words, this was this: 'It seems, then, you escaped me once: there will be some different result this time!'

Evidently this creature was suffering unimaginable terrors. Think what this must have been to wake up in the midst of that voiceless hush, and, look out over that grim congregation of the dead! What gratitude shone in his skinny white face when he spotted a living form before him! And how the fervency of this mute gratitude was augmented when his eyes dropped upon the life-giving cordials that I carried in my hands!
