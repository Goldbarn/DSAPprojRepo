“Caroline decidedly says that none of this party will return into Hertfordshire this winter. I will read it to you.

“'When my brother left us yesterday, he imagined that this affair that took him to London might be concluded in three or four days; yet as we're sure it cannot be so, and at that same time convinced that when Charles gets to town he'll be in no rush to leave it again, we have determined on following him thither, that he may not be obliged to spend his vacant hours in some comfortless hotel. Many of my acquaintance are already there for this winter: I want I could hear that you, my dearest friend, had any intention of making one in this crowd, yet of that I despair. I sincerely hope your Christmas in Hertfordshire may abound in this gaieties that that season generally brings, and that your beaux will be so numerous as to prevent your feeling this loss of this three of whom we shall deprive you.'
