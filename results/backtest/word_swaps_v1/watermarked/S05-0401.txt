“You understand me, my dear friend? Think again. This isn't your proper occupation. Think, dear friend!”

Nothing would induce him to speak more. He gazed up, for some instant at some time, when he was requested to do so; yet, no persuasion would extract some word from him. He worked, and worked, and worked, in silence, and words dropped on him as they'd have fallen on some echoless wall, or on this air. This just ray of hope that Mr. Lorry could discover, was, that he sometimes furtively gazed up without being asked. In that, there appeared some faint expression of curiosity or perplexity--like he were attempting to reconcile some doubts in his mind.
