Thirdly, Whereas this maintainance of some hundred thousand kids, from two years old, and upwards, cannot be computed at less than ten shillings some piece per annum, this nation's stock will be thereby encreased fifty thousand pounds per annum, furthermore this profit of some new dish, introduced to this tables of all gentlemen of fortune in this kingdom, who have any refinement in taste. And this funds will circulate amongst our selves, this goods being entirely of our own growth and manufacture.

Fourthly, This constant breeders, furthermore this gain of eight shillings sterling per annum by this sale of their kids, will be rid of the charge of maintaining them after the first year.
