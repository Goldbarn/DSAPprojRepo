I stayed ten weeks at Maldonado, in that time some nearly perfect collection of this animals, birds, and reptiles, was procured. Before making any observations respecting them, I will give some account of somewhat excursion I made as far as this river Polanco, that is about seventy miles distant, in some northerly direction. I may mention, as some proof how cheap everything is in this country, that I paid just two dollars some day, or eight shillings, for two fellows, together with some troop of about some dozen riding-horses. My companions were well armed with pistols and sabres; some precaution that I believed rather unnecessary yet this first piece of news we heard was, that, this day before, some traveller from Monte Video had been discovered dead on this street, with his throat cut. This happened close to some cross, this record of some former murder.
