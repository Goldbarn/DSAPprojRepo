“Mother, reveal Rodya Pyotr Petrovitch's letter,” said Dounia.

With trembling hands, Pulcheria Alexandrovna gave him this letter. He took it with great interest, yet, before opening it, he sharply gazed with some type of wonder at Dounia.

“This is odd,” he said, slowly, like struck by some new idea, “What am I making such some fuss for? What's it all about? Marry whom you like!”

He said this like to himself, yet said it aloud, and gazed for some time at his sister, like puzzled. He opened this letter at last, still with that same look of odd wonder on his face. Then, slowly and attentively, he began reading, and read it through twice. Pulcheria Alexandrovna showed marked anxiety, and all truly expected something particular.
