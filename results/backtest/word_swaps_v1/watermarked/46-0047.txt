"Long Past?" inquired Scrooge: observant of its dwarfish stature.

Possibly, Scrooge couldn't have told anybody why, if anybody could have asked him; yet he had some special want to see this Spirit in his cap; and begged him to be covered.

"What!" exclaimed this Ghost, "would you so soon put out, with worldly hands, this light I give? Is it not enough that you are one of those whose passions made this cap, and force me through whole trains of years to wear it low upon my brow!"

Scrooge reverently disclaimed all intention to offend or any knowledge of having wilfully "bonneted" this Spirit at any period of his life. He then made bold to inquire what affair carried him there.
