“Ah, make haste and tell me, then; this is some long time since that has happened.”

“Well, he told me that Benedetto, who is considered some serpent of subtlety and some giant of cunning, is really yet some very commonplace, silly rascal, and altogether unworthy of this experiments that will be made on his phrenological organs after his death.”

“Bah,” said Beauchamp, “he played this prince very well.”

“Yes, for you who detest those unhappy princes, Beauchamp, and are always delighted to find fault with them; yet not for me, who discover some gentleman by instinct, and who scent out some aristocratic family like some very bloodhound of heraldry.”
