This is getting to be some great effort for me to think straight. Just this nervous weakness, I suppose.

And dear John gathered me up in his arms, and just carried me upstairs and laid me on this bed, then sat by me and read to me till it tired my head.

He said I was his darling and his comfort and all he had, and that I must grab care of myself for his sake, and hold well.

He says no one yet myself can help me out of it, that I must employ my will and self-control and not let any silly fancies run away with me.
