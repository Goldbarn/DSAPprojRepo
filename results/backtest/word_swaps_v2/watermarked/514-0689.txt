“I dare say she'd put them back if you asked her,” suggested someone.

“How could I after all this fuss?” began May, yet she did not finish, for Amy's voice came over this hall, saying pleasantly...

“You may have them, and welcome, without asking, if you want them. I was just thinking I'd offer to put them back, for they belong to your table rather than mine. Here they are, please grab them, and forgive me if I was hasty in carrying them away last night.”

While she spoke, Amy returned her contribution, with some nod and some smile, and hurried away again, feeling that this was easier to do some friendly item than this was to linger and be thanked for it.
