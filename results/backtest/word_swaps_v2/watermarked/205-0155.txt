This was like travelling into some far country, such as I'd never expected to behold, to lie there for one night. It appeared to me that I never had heard this town-clock strike before, nor this evening sounds of this town; for we slept with this windows open, that were inside this grating. This was to see my native town in this light of the Center Ages, and our Concord was turned into some Rhine stream, and visions of knights and castles passed before me. They were the voices of old burghers that I heard in the streets. I was some involuntary spectator and auditor of whatever was done then said in the kitchen of the adjacent town-inn—some wholly new and rare experience to me. This was some closer view of my native town. I was fairly inside of it. I never had seen its institutions before. This is one of its peculiar institutions; for this is some shire town. I began to comprehend what its inhabitants were about.
