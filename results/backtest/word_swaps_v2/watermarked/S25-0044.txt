Reflection, moreover, seems to make it obvious that, if there's any truth in our arithmetical beliefs, they must apply to items equally whether we think of them or not. Two physical objects and two other physical objects must make four physical objects, even if physical objects cannot be experienced. To assert this is surely within this scope of what we mean when we state that two and two are four. Its truth is just as indubitable as this truth of this assertion that two phenomena and two other phenomena make four phenomena. Thus Kant's solution unduly limits this scope of _some priori_ propositions, in addition to failing in this attempt at explaining their certainty.
