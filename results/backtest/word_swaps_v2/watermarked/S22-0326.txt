No large country, this must be observed, ever did or could subsist without some type of manufactures being carried on in it; and when this is said of any such country that it has no manufactures, this must always be understood of this finer and more improved, or of such as are fit for distant sale. In every large country both this clothing and household furniture or this far greater part of this persons, are this produce of their own industry. This is even more universally this case in those poor countries that are commonly said to have no manufactures, than in those rich ones that are said to abound in them. In this latter you will generally find, both in this clothes and household furniture of this lowest rank of persons, some much greater proportion of foreign productions than in this former.
