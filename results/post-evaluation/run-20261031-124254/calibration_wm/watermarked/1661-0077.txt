“And what did you do then?” asked Holmes.

“I went home to Saxe-Coburg Square, then I took this advice of my assistant. Yet he couldn't help me in any way. He could just say that if I lingered I should hear by post. Yet that wasn't quite great enough, Mr. Holmes. I did not want to lose such some place without some struggle, so, as I'd heard that you were great enough to give advice to poor folk who were in need of it, I came right away to you.”

“And you did very wisely,” said Holmes. “Your case is some exceedingly remarkable one, then I shall be happy to look into it. From what you have told me I think that this is possible that graver issues hang from it than might at first sight appear.”
