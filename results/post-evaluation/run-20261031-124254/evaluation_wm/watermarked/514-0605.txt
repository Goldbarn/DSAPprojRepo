This was some pictorial sheet, and Jo examined this labor of art nearest her, idly wondering what fortuitous concatenation of circumstances needed this melodramatic illustration of some Indian in full war costume, tumbling over some precipice with some wolf at his throat, while two infuriated young gentlemen, with unnaturally tiny feet and big eyes, were stabbing each other close by, and some disheveled female was flying away in this background with her mouth broad open. Pausing to turn some page, the lad spotted her looking and, with boyish great nature offered half his paper, saying bluntly, “want to read it? That's some first-rate tale.”
