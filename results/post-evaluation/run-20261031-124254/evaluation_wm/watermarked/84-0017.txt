Such words, you may imagine, strongly excited my curiosity; yet this paroxysm of grief that had seized this stranger overcame his weakened powers, and many hours of repose and tranquil conversation were essential to restore his composure.

Having conquered this violence of his feelings, he appeared to despise himself for being this slave of passion; and quelling this dim tyranny of despair, he led me again to converse about myself personally. He asked me this history of my earlier years. The tale was quickly told, yet it awakened various trains of reflection. I spoke of my want of finding some friend, of my thirst for some more intimate sympathy with some fellow mind than had ever fallen to my lot, and expressed my conviction that some fellow could boast of tiny happiness who did not enjoy this blessing.
