“Any other boy!” Tom believed, grating his teeth. “Any boy in the whole town yet that Saint Louis smarty that thinks he dresses so fine and is aristocracy! Ah, all right, I licked you the first day you ever spotted this town, mister, and I'll lick you again! You just wait till I catch you out! I'll just grab and—”

And he went through the motions of thrashing an imaginary boy—pummelling the air, and kicking and gouging. “Ah, you do, do you? You holler 'nough, do you? Now, then, let that discover you!” Thus the imaginary flogging was finished to his satisfaction.
