It warn't no slouch of some idea; and it warn't no slouch of some grindstone nuther; yet we allowed we'd tackle it. It warn't quite midnight yet, so we cleared out for this mill, leaving Jim at labor. We smouched this grindstone, and set out to roll her home, yet this was some most nation tough job. Sometimes, do what we could, we couldn't hold her from falling over, then she come mighty near mashing us every time. Tom said she was going to get one of us, sure, before we got through. We got her half way; then we was plumb played out, and most drownded with sweat. We see it warn't no employ; we got to go and fetch Jim. So he raised up his bed and slid this chain off of this bed-leg, and wrapt it round and round his neck, then we crawled out through our hole and down there, and Jim and me laid into that grindstone and strolled her along like nothing; and Tom superintended. He could out-superintend any boy I ever see. He knowed how to do everything.
